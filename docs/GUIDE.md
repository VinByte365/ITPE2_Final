# User Guide

## Faculty

1. **Register & approval.** Register at `/register` as faculty. An admin must approve
   your account before you can sign in.
2. **Create a subject.** From `/faculty`, click "New subject". Set name, code, term,
   school year, passing percent (50 or 75) and enrollment open/closed. Share the
   join code with students.
3. **Add topics.** Open a subject → Topics. Add one topic (title + available-from
   date) or bulk-create 10–16 topics at once with a start date and meeting days.
   Topics open on their scheduled date; future topics are grayed out for students.
4. **Add content.** Open a topic → add text/markdown blocks, upload presentations
   or files, and reorder blocks.
5. **Build a quiz.** Open a topic → Quiz. Add 10–20 multiple-choice (4 options) or
   text-input questions, or import via CSV. Publish when ready. For an uploaded
   quiz, set mode to "uploaded" and let students submit a score image.
6. **Grade & review.** Grading queue shows pending submissions. Type the score for
   uploaded quizzes. Built-in quizzes are auto-graded.
7. **Reports.** Subject report → preview, sort by surname/score, print, download
   PDF/Excel/CSV. Per-student scores page shows all quizzes for one student.
8. **Progress.** Progress view shows each student's topic/quiz state.

Changing passing % re-computes pass/fail everywhere. Publishing or re-grading a
quiz may re-lock students who no longer pass.

## Students

1. **Register & join.** Register at `/register` as student (student number
   required). Join a subject with the code from your faculty.
2. **Learn.** Open a subject → topics. Only the current/open topic is accessible;
   future topics are grayed out; past topics stay readable.
3. **Read content.** Open a topic to read text, view slides/files.
4. **Take quizzes.** Open a topic's quiz, answer all questions, submit. You must
   pass to unlock the next topic; otherwise repeat the topic.
5. **Uploaded quizzes.** Upload an image of your score; faculty grades it manually.
6. **Grades.** View your per-subject grades and overall average.
